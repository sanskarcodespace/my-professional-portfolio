// About Me

Who I Am

Sanskar Sharma

Full Stack Developer & App Builder 📍 Patna, Bihar, India 🎓 B.Tech CSE · Manav Rachna · 2029 ⚡ Product Intern @ PixiePlot

"I believe the best products are built at the intersection of design, technology, and ambition."

01

Where It Started

I fell in love with coding the moment I realised that writing a few lines could make something happen on screen. Growing up in Patna, I didn't have a tech ecosystem around me — just curiosity, a laptop, and the internet. That was enough. I started with Python, broke things constantly, and learned faster than I ever did in a classroom.

02

What I'm Building Now

At PixiePlot, I'm not just writing code — I'm learning what it means to build a product people actually use. From feature planning to deployment, every cycle teaches me something a tutorial never could. I ship HTML, CSS, JS, Python, and Flask — every single day — and I love the challenge of making something real from scratch.

03

Where I'm Going

I want to build companies. AI-driven startups that make people's lives measurably better. I'm not waiting for the "right time" — I'm building skills, shipping projects, and thinking like a founder right now. By the time I graduate, I want to have already started something worth continuing.
