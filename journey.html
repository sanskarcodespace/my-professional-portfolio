THE JOURNEY

From curiosity to craft. From ideas to impact.

01
The Spark

The First Line of Code

It started with pure curiosity — no roadmap, no mentor, just me and a blinking cursor. The moment I ran my first Python script and saw it actually do something, I was hooked. That feeling of making a machine obey a thought has never left me.

print("Hello, World!")
02
The Foundation

Learning to Build

I moved from Python to HTML, CSS, JavaScript — building web pages that actually looked like things. Each project was messier than the last, and each one taught me something no tutorial covered. I learned to embrace the broken builds and love the debugging.

03
The Leap

Entering the Real World

B.Tech at Manav Rachna gave me structure. PixiePlot gave me stakes. Working on a real product — with real users — changed everything. I stopped thinking like a student and started thinking like someone who has to ship.

🎓 B.Tech CSE
⚡ PixiePlot Intern
04
The Build

Shipping Real Products

Building the Auto Payment Reminder App was my first "real" product — one that's live, deployed, and actually does what it promises. That first Vercel deployment URL hit different. The gap between "I built this" and "this is live" is everything.

💳 Auto Payment Reminder
Live on Vercel
05
The Vision

What's Coming

More products. Deeper AI integration. Founding something that lasts. The plan isn't to wait until I'm "ready" — it's to keep building until I look back and realise I already started.

✦ ✦ ✦
"I don't wait for the future.
I build it."

I believe ambition without action is just noise. So I code, I ship, I learn, and I repeat. Every project is a step toward the company I'll found — and the impact I'll make.
